Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

The short version: your photos, rosters and captions stay on your computer. The app has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It only goes online to check your licence, look for updates, and download things you ask for.

This policy explains what information Metadata Express (operated by Adam Obirek, "we", "us") handles, through the Mac app and this website.

What stays on your computer

Your photos, the metadata and captions you write, your rosters, score sheets, settings, your tagging-speed stats and AI Suggest's face and number data are stored only on your computer. We never receive them. The app keeps local files (settings, licence status, and AI Suggest logs used to measure accuracy) in your user folder; you can delete them anytime.

What the app sends, and why

  • Licence activation and checks go to Lemon Squeezy: your licence key, and a name for the activation made from your computer's name and operating system (for example "Jamies-MacBook (macOS)"), so you can see and manage which computers use your subscription. The app re-checks the key about once a day when online.
  • Update checks download a small file describing the latest version from GitHub, and updates themselves are downloaded from GitHub. Like any download, GitHub receives your IP address and the app's version.
  • AI Suggest (optional) downloads its AI model once from Hugging Face. After that it runs entirely on your computer.
  • Roster import (optional) loads the web page you enter, from that website.

The app doesn't send your photos, file names, metadata, rosters or usage statistics anywhere.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record. They collect your payment details, name, email address and billing address under their privacy policy. We receive your name, email address, order and subscription details, but never your full card number.

Contact form and suggestions

If you use our contact form (including feature suggestions), we receive your message, your email address, and your name, phone number and role if you give them. They're delivered to us by email through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service. We use them only to read your message and reply to you.

Email

When you contact us, we use your message and email address to reply and help you. We may email subscribers about their subscription or important changes to the app. We don't sell or rent email addresses.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no analytics, cookies or trackers of our own. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

How we use and share information

We use the information above only to provide and support Metadata Express: selling and checking subscriptions, preventing licence sharing, delivering updates and answering questions. We share it only with the service providers named here, or when required by law. We don't sell personal information.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep purchase and licence records for as long as you have a subscription and afterwards as needed for tax and legal reasons. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information through our contact form; where we can't delete something (for example tax records), we'll explain why.

Children

Metadata Express is for professional and adult users and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

We'll post any changes to this policy here with a new effective date, and tell subscribers about significant changes.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: use our contact form.
