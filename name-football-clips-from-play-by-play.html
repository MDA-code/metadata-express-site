Name football clips from the game's play-by-play

Game Book (beta) in Label Clips: the play-by-play PDF names your clips. Mac and Windows.

After a football game you have a card full of C0001.MP4, C0002.MP4… and a game book that already says what happened on every play. Matching them by hand means scrolling the play-by-play for each clip. Game Book in Metadata Express does the matching: add the PDF, and every clip comes up with its likeliest plays under the name box.

From the game book to the file name

The play-by-play says:

2-6 TIG31 (13:02) #12 D.Hale pass complete short right to #8 K.Brooks for 18 yards to the TIG49, 1ST DOWN.

You press ⌘1 (Ctrl+1 on Windows), and the clip is named:

031426_HALE_PASS_COMPLETE_TO_BROOKS_FOR_18_YARDS.MP4

How it works

  1. Add the game book. In Label Clips, click Game Book and choose the game's play-by-play PDF.
  2. MDX lines up clips and plays. It compares when your clips were recorded with the game clock, so it works even when the camera's clock is off.
  3. Pick the play. The three likeliest plays show under the name box: ⌘1, ⌘2 or ⌘3 puts one in, and More Plays shows the ones around it. Every pick lines up the clips after it.
  4. Rename and go on. + renames the clip and shows the next one.

Worded your way

  • Label Style: type how you'd name one run, one pass, one tackle, and every play of that kind is worded the same way.
  • Your players first: with your roster loaded, a tackle is named for your defender, not the other team's runner.
  • The other team, written the way you write it, changes with each game.
  • Name Format adds the date, shooter, game and clip number, with underscores or dashes and ALL CAPS.
  • Templates share all of it with your team.

Clips that aren't plays (fans, the band, a pre-snap ISO) are named the usual way: a jersey number and Enter for the player, your own codes for the rest. See labeling clips by player.

Questions

What is a game book?

The official stats PDF from a game, with the play-by-play: every play's quarter, clock, players and yards. Most college programs post it with the box score after the game.

How does it know which play a clip is?

By timing. It compares the rhythm of your clips (when each was recorded) with the rhythm of the plays on the game clock, so the camera's clock doesn't have to be right. Each play you pick lines up the clips after it.

Which game books work?

Football game books in the NCAA LiveStats layout, with pages titled “Play By Play report”. More layouts and sports are coming. Game Book is in beta.

Can the names follow my team's naming convention?

Yes. Label Style words each kind of play your way, and Name Format adds the date, shooter, game and clip number with underscores and ALL CAPS if you use them.
