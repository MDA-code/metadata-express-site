Installing on a Mac

  1. Open the downloaded Metadata-Express.dmg and drag Metadata Express into Applications.
  2. Open Metadata Express from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app: click Done (not Move to Trash).
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Metadata Express, click Open Anyway, and enter your Mac password.

You only do this once; after that it opens like any other app and updates itself.

Installing on Windows

  1. Open the downloaded Metadata-Express-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the installer. No administrator password is needed, and Metadata Express is added to the Start menu.

You only do this once; after that it updates itself.

Why I built this

I'm a photographer and videographer for a sports team in New Jersey, and I've been shooting sports since 2017. I got into creative work in college, when I found out engineering just wasn't for me and I wanted something that would activate the creative part of my brain. Photography and video do that for me.

We used to tag all our photos after exporting them, on an athlete delivery app. Tagging the players took forever. When we switched to Greenfly, we found out it could put a photo in the right player's gallery if the keywords in the metadata included the player's name exactly as it is on the Greenfly roster. We tried other apps, but it was still tedious and time consuming.

So I built this for our team over the summer, as a fun project during the long days of training camp. There were plenty of issues at first, since I'm not a software developer. I used Claude to help me, because that was the only way I could get it to do what I needed.

Once the metadata part was working, we wanted to see how far we could take it. So we kept adding the features that help our own workflow: importing straight from the card, culling, and sending photos to our editors. If it helps us, we figure it can help others too.

After six weeks of our in-house photographers, editors, interns and me using it every game, I wanted to share it. It saves us a lot of time after games, and adding metadata is honestly more enjoyable now. In a timed test, I tagged 1,000 photos an hour faster than with the tool we used before.

The subscription isn't about getting rich. Building and keeping something like this running has real costs: development tools, the website, payment processing and the updates that keep it working. The 14-day free trial is there so you can decide for yourself if it's worth it.

Questions

Does it use AI?

Only if you want it to. Tagging, captions, culling and Lightroom transfer use no AI: you type a number and it looks the name up in your own roster. AI Suggest is optional, off by default, and runs only on your computer. If your clients require no AI at all, download the Non AI version: it doesn't include any AI software, and it updates only to Non AI versions.

Do my photos or clips get uploaded anywhere?

No. Metadata Express works on the photos and video clips on your computer and writes the metadata into the files themselves. Your files, rosters and captions never leave your computer unless you send them.

How do I make a roster?

It's a simple text file: one line per athlete with their jersey number and name. Follow our step-by-step roster guide (with examples to download), build one in the app (click the i next to Roster in Session Setup), or let Metadata Express make one from the team's roster page.

Can it send photos to my editors or social team?

Yes. Click Send… in Import / Cull and add a destination: your Dropbox, Google Drive, Box or OneDrive folder (found automatically; their app uploads it), or an FTP / SFTP server from your outlet or team. Photos go in the background, JPEGs only if you like, and can go automatically right after you import a card. Passwords are kept in your computer's keychain.

Will it run on my computer?

Mac: it needs Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4 or newer) and macOS 13 Ventura or later. To check, open the Apple menu → About This Mac: “Chip” should say Apple M… and the macOS version should be 13 or higher. Intel Macs aren't supported. Windows: 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. AI Suggest also needs 16 GB of memory (and on Windows an NVIDIA graphics card with 8 GB of video memory); everything else runs on any supported computer. Try it free for 14 days to be sure.

Which files does it work with?

JPEG and RAW files from every major camera: Sony, Canon, Nikon, Fujifilm, OM System / Olympus, Panasonic, Pentax, Ricoh, Leica, Hasselblad, Phase One, Sigma and DNG. Tags and captions go inside JPEGs and into an .xmp file next to each RAW, which Lightroom and other photo software read.

Does it work with video?

Yes, with Label Clips, now in beta: it works today and we're still making it better. It plays your clips (MXF, MOV and MP4 from Sony, Canon, Panasonic, Fujifilm, Nikon, phones and GoPro, including XAVC, ProRes, DNxHD and HEVC) and lets you rename each one as you watch: a jersey number and Enter puts in the player, your own codes add the play. Mark In / Out and export your selects as a timeline for Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve. Camera RAW video (BRAW, R3D) can be renamed and sent, but needs the camera maker's software to play.

Will it work with the platform my team uses?

Metadata Express writes standard IPTC and XMP keywords and captions, the same fields Lightroom, wire services and most photo-sharing and athlete-delivery platforms read.

Can it rename my files with player names?

Yes. In Session Setup, tick Rename photos with player names when saving and choose a style: HaleMarcus1, Hale_Marcus_1, HALE_1, MarcusHale1 or Marcus_Hale_1. Photos with several players get every name (MarcusHale_DevinBrooks1), numbers continue from what's already in the folder so nothing is overwritten, and the tags and captions are still written as usual.

Will it overwrite the IPTC template I already use?

No. Metadata Express only writes the caption, keywords, the people shown and the template fields you fill in. Everything else already in your photos (byline, location, instructions, ratings, camera data) is kept. You can also build your full template right in the app.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. It needs a connection once every 7 days to confirm your subscription, and once to download AI Suggest if you turn it on.

Can I use it on my laptop and my desktop?

Yes. Each subscription works on 2 computers. You can move it to a new computer anytime from About & License > Deactivate.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Open Manage subscription (or About & License › Manage Subscription in the app) and sign in with the email you subscribed with. You can switch between monthly and yearly, update your card, or cancel. If you cancel, you keep using Metadata Express until the end of the period you paid for and won't be charged again. See our refund policy.

Is there a Windows version?

Yes. Download for Windows above. Your subscription's 2 computers can be any mix of Mac and Windows.
