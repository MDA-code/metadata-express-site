Installing on a Mac

  1. Open the downloaded Metadata-Express.dmg and drag Metadata Express into Applications.
  2. Open Metadata Express from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app: click Done (not Move to Trash).
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Metadata Express, click Open Anyway, and enter your Mac password.

You only do this once; after that it opens like any other app and updates itself.

Installing on Windows

  1. Open the downloaded Metadata-Express-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the installer. No administrator password is needed, and Metadata Express is added to the Start menu.

You only do this once; after that it updates itself.

Mac: Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13 Ventura or later. AI Suggest needs 16 GB of memory and a one-time ~6 GB download.

Windows: 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. AI Suggest needs 16 GB of memory, an NVIDIA graphics card with 8 GB of video memory, and a one-time ~6 GB download.

Questions

Does it use AI?

Only if you want it to. Tagging, captions, culling and Lightroom transfer use no AI: you type a number and it looks the name up in your own roster. AI Suggest is optional, off by default, and runs only on your computer. If your clients require no AI at all, download the No AI version: it doesn't include any AI software, and it updates only to No AI versions.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Metadata Express works on the photos on your computer and writes the metadata into the files themselves. Your photos, rosters and captions never leave your computer.

How do I make a roster?

It's a simple text file: one line per athlete with their jersey number and name. Follow our step-by-step roster guide (with examples to download), build one in the app (click the i next to Roster in Session Setup), or let Metadata Express make one from the team's roster page.

Can it send photos to my editors or social team?

Yes. Click Send… in Import / Cull and add a destination: your Dropbox, Google Drive, Box or OneDrive folder (found automatically; their app uploads it), or an FTP / SFTP server from your outlet or team. Photos go in the background, JPEGs only if you like, and can go automatically right after you import a card. Passwords are kept in your computer's keychain.

Which files does it work with?

Tagging and captions work with JPEG files. Cull mode opens JPEG and RAW files from Sony, Canon, Nikon and Fujifilm cameras (.ARW, .CR3, .CR2, .NEF, .RAF).

Will it work with the platform my team uses?

Metadata Express writes standard IPTC and XMP keywords and captions, the same fields Lightroom, wire services and most photo-sharing and athlete-delivery platforms read.

Can it rename my files with player names?

Yes. In Session Setup, tick Rename photos with player names when saving and choose a style: DuffJrKJ1, DuffJr_KJ_1, DUFFJR_1, KJDuffJr1 or KJ_DuffJr_1. Photos with several players get every name (KJDuffJr_JesseOfurie1), numbers continue from what's already in the folder so nothing is overwritten, and the tags and captions are still written as usual.

Will it overwrite the IPTC template I already use?

No. Metadata Express only writes the caption, keywords, the people shown and the template fields you fill in. Everything else already in your photos (byline, location, instructions, ratings, camera data) is kept. You can also build your full template right in the app.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. It needs a connection once every 7 days to confirm your subscription, and once to download AI Suggest if you turn it on.

Can I use it on my laptop and my desktop?

Yes. Each subscription works on 2 computers. You can move it to a new computer anytime from About & License > Deactivate.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Open Manage subscription (or About & License › Manage Subscription in the app) and sign in with the email you subscribed with. You can switch between monthly and yearly, update your card, or cancel. If you cancel, you keep using Metadata Express until the end of the period you paid for and won't be charged again. See our refund policy.

Yes. Download for Windows above. Your subscription's 2 computers can be any mix of Mac and Windows.
