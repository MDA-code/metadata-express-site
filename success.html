Getting your license key…

Your license key

Save it somewhere safe. It's also printed on your future invoices from Stripe. It works on up to 2 computers.

Activate Metadata Express

  1. Open Metadata Express. If you don't have it yet, request the download.
  2. On the Home screen, click the faint Free trial · … days left in the bottom-left corner (it opens About & License).
  3. Paste your key and click Activate.

To use it on a second computer, do the same there. To move it to a new computer later, click Deactivate on the old one first.
