Keep it somewhere safe: email it to yourself or save the file. It's also printed on your future invoices, and if you ever lose it, contact us and we'll send it again. It works on up to 2 computers.

Activate Metadata Express

  1. Open Metadata Express. If you don't have it yet, download it here (install steps are on our home page).
  2. On the Home screen, click the faint Free trial · … days left in the bottom-left corner (it opens About & License).
  3. Paste your key and click Activate.

To use it on a second computer, do the same there. To move it to a new computer later, click Deactivate on the old one first.
